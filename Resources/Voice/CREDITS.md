# Sound credits

Every file here is from an open-source pack whose license allows redistribution. Changes for Warden:
each file was mixed to mono, matched to -18 LUFS with peaks limited to -1.5 dBFS, converted to AAC,
and in the Kenney packs, `war_hold` or `hold`, `war_look_out`, and `war_call_for_backup` were renamed
`hold`, `look_out`, and `call_for_backup`.

## kenney-female/ and kenney-male/

Voiceover Pack #1 by Kenney Vleugels (www.kenney.nl), female voice by Giselle, male voice by
Jeffrey M. Smith. Dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Files: ready, call_for_backup, hold, mission_completed, objective_achieved, congratulations,
mission_failed, wrong, time_over, hurry_up, look_out. Each says its name.

## aimee/

Announcer Voice Pack, General Phrases. Voice clips recorded and performed by Aimee Smith
(www.aimeesmithva.com). Licensed under CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Source: https://opengameart.org/content/announcer-voice-pack-general-phrases-female

Files: ready, continue, clear, congratulations, fail, try_again, time_up. Each says its name.

## dings/

minimal-dings by iain, version 1.0.1, synthesized chimes dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0:
https://github.com/iain/minimal-dings

Files: input (bing-bong), ack (single tone), start (rising fifth), end (falling fifth),
complete (rising fourth), error (descending triad), limit (three steady tones), spam (three dry tones).
